Easy Chicken Enchiladas

From diabeticlivingonline.com
⏱ Prep 10 min
🍲 Slow cooker 4–5 hrs
🍽 Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb skinless boneless chicken breast
  • 1 (10½ oz) can cream of chicken soup
  • ½ cup mild salsa
  • 1 (4 oz) can diced green chiles
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • 8 corn tortillas, warmed
  • ½ cup shredded Mexican 4-cheese blend
  • Baby lettuce mix (bed)

Method

  1. 1Place chicken in a 1½-qt slow cooker. Mix soup, salsa, chiles, chili powder & cumin; pour over chicken. Cover, cook on LOW 4–5 hrs.
  2. 2Shred chicken with two forks. Toss with half the sauce.
  3. 3Line a platter with lettuce. Roll ~⅓ cup chicken + a little cheese into each tortilla. Arrange, spoon on remaining sauce, sprinkle cheese. Serve.
