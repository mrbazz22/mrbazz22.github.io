Mozzarella-Stuffed Chicken Parm

From delish.com
⏱ Prep 20 min
🔥 Bake ~20 min
🍽 Serves 3–4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts
  • 8 oz fresh mozzarella
  • Kosher salt & black pepper
  • 1 cup flour
  • 3 eggs, beaten
  • 1 cup panko bread crumbs
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ cup freshly grated Parmesan, divided
  • Olive oil, for frying
  • 2 cups marinara
  • ¼ cup fresh basil, sliced
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley

Method

  1. 1Preheat oven to 425°. Cut a deep slit in each breast; stuff with mozzarella, press to seal. Season outside with salt & pepper.
  2. 2Set up 3 bowls: flour, eggs, panko. Whisk garlic powder, oregano, ¼ cup Parmesan & ½ tsp salt into panko.
  3. 3Dredge chicken in flour, then egg, then panko.
  4. 4In a large skillet over medium, heat a thin layer of olive oil. Brown chicken ~4 min/side until golden. Pour marinara around chicken, scatter basil over sauce. Sprinkle remaining Parmesan on top.
  5. 5Transfer skillet to oven; bake until cooked through, ~20 min. Garnish with parsley.
