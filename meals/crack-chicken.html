Crack Chicken Casserole

From cookingprofessionally.com
⏱ Prep 30 min
🔥 Bake 30 min
🍽 Serves 5–6

Ingredients

  • 1 box chicken Rice-A-Roni (or rice & pasta mix)
  • 1 cup carrots, thinly sliced
  • ½ cup green pepper, chopped
  • ½ cup onion, finely diced
  • 1 (10½ oz) can cream of chicken soup
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 2 cups cooked chicken breast, chopped
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ cup shredded sharp cheddar

Method

  1. 1Preheat oven to 350°. Grease a 9×13 baking dish.
  2. 2Cook Rice-A-Roni per package. In the last 5 min, add green pepper, carrots & onion.
  3. 3Stir in cream of chicken soup & sour cream.
  4. 4Add chicken breast & black pepper. Remove from heat.
  5. 5Pour into dish; sprinkle with cheddar. Bake 30 min. Serve.
